Sound Sync — WiFi Audio Relay

Connect to your surroundings and stream studio-grade audio to any device over WiFi with sub-10ms latency.

High-Fidelity Wireless Sound Streaming Across Devices

Sound Sync is an open-source, web-based wireless audio relay system developed by VIK-V-VKS. It allows users to stream real-time audio from personal computers (browser tabs, YouTube, Spotify, desktop sound, or microphones) directly to mobile phones, tablets, and secondary speakers over local WiFi with physical latency between 2ms and 10ms.

Unlike legacy Bluetooth protocols that incur 150ms to 250ms of audio delay and lossy compression, Sound Sync utilizes peer-to-peer WebRTC data channels, the Opus interactive speech and audio codec at 48,000 Hz stereo, and the HTML5 Web Audio API to deliver synchronized, lossless sound across multiple rooms.

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Ultra-Low Latency: Physical transit delay under 10ms over local WiFi networks.
  • Universal Browser Compatibility: Zero installation required. Runs directly in Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.
  • Multi-Device Broadcast: Transmit to multiple phones and tablets simultaneously for shared listening, silent disco, or multi-room party sound.
  • Full Stereo 48kHz Opus Encoding: Studio-quality frequency response from 20Hz to 20,000Hz.
  • Zero Cloud Audio Storage: All audio content travels strictly peer-to-peer (P2P) and is encrypted end-to-end via DTLS/SRTP.
